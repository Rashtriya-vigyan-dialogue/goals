01 / What is RVD?

A space for ideas
to meet institutions.

RVD brings together research-oriented thinking and public-policy questions. It approaches governance not as a collection of slogans, but as a set of systems that can be examined, questioned and improved.

A student-led public policy and research collective committed to first-principle thinking, technology-facilitated governance and empathetic public policy.

Rashtriya Vigyan Dialogue (RVD) is a platform for exploring the relationship between science, institutions, public administration and society. Its work spans policy proposals, research-led commentary, institutional design and public dialogue.

The Dialogue is interested in the questions that sit between disciplines: how public systems can use technology responsibly; how evidence can inform decisions; how institutions can become more responsive; and how development can remain attentive to people and the environment.

RVD's public work includes its declaration of goals, policy demands, editorial writing and digital resources. The site brings these strands together so readers can understand the collective, explore its work and engage with the ideas behind it.

“Independent thought. Informed discussion. Constructive action.”
